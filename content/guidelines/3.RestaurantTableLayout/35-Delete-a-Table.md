1\.	Press the **Edit** button at the top right.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/chapter33.png>)

2\.	Select the table you want to delete.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/44.png>)

3\.	Press the **Delete** button to permanently delete the table.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/33.png>)

4\.	Press **Save** to save your changes.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/22.png>)

5\.	You have successfully deleted a table.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/11.png>)
