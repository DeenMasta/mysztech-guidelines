You can use Cash Drawer Logs to view the cash drawer activity recorded in the system.

1\.	Go to **Management Hub > Logs > Cash Drawer Logs**.You can view a list of all **Cash Drawer Logs**.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-095825.png>)

2\. Use the **Search Bar** to search for a log by **POS Device, Staff Name, or Activity.**

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-095940.png>)

3\.	You can also click the **Filter** icon to filter the logs by **POS Device, Date From, and Date Until.**

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-100033.png>)

4\.	After selecting the required filters, check the details and click **Apply Filters** to view the results.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-100110.png>)
