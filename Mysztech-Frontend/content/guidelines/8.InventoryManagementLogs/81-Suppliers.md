You can manage your outlet’s suppliers from the **Suppliers** page. This setup needs to be done in the **Management Hub.**

Don’t know how to access Management Hub ?[ *Click Here to learn how to access Management Hub.*](http://localhost:5173/docs?id=How-to-access-Management-Hub)

1\.	Go to **Inventory > Suppliers**. This page will display all the registered suppliers.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/1.png>)

2\.	Click **New Supplier** to create a new supplier.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/8.1.1.png>)

3\.	Enter the supplier’s **Name** and **Business Registration Number.**

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/2.png>)

4\.	Enter their **Email** and **Phone Number.**

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/3.png>)

5\.	Select the **Settlement Type:**

o	**Cash**

o	**Credit**

o	**N/A** if other types apply.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/4.png>)

6\.	Optionally, enter the supplier’s **Address** and add any notes in the **Description** box.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/5.png>)

7\.	Select the **Outlet** that the supplier supplies.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/6.png>)

8\.	Check the details and click **Create.**

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/7.png>)

9\.	Your supplier has been successfully created.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/8.1.2.png>)

10\.	You can also use the **Search** button to search for a supplier by name.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/8.1.3.png>)

11\.	The **Column Adjustment** button allows you to choose which columns to display, such as **Name, Email, and Phone Number**. Click **Apply Columns** to save your selection.

![](</admin/8.Invenory Management Logs/8.2/8.1.4.png>)
