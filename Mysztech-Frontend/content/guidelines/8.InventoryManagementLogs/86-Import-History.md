Import History allows you to check the files that were imported into the system, such as files imported from the **Items** section.

1\.	Go to **Logs > Import History**. You can view a list of all imported files.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-102613.png>)

2\. Use the Search Bar to search for an import.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-102646.png>)

3\. Click the Filter button to filter the import history.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-102744.png>)

4\. Filter the import history by **Import Type** and **Status**. You can enable or disable **Has Failures** to show or hide imports that have failed items. Check the selected filters and click **Apply Filters** to view the results.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-102849.png>)

5\.	Click the **Columns** button, then tick the boxes for the columns you want to show. Columns that are not selected will not be shown in the list.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-102928.png>)

6\.	The selected columns will appear in the import history list. You can click on the list for more detail.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-103017.png>)

7\.	Here you can view the detail information import and you can click the download failed rows to download the detail.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-28-103258.png>)
